# Idea helper

1. Clarify the desired outcome and one important constraint.
2. Generate a small varied set of ideas.
3. Remove duplicates and ideas that violate the constraint.
4. Compare effort, usefulness, risk, and speed to first result.
5. Recommend one idea with one reason.
6. Turn the selected idea into one reversible next step and a visible proof.

Do not overwhelm a beginner with a long list. Default to three ideas; provide
more only when requested.
