# ProblemOS Lite

Use one compact capsule:

- `P` — what is actually wrong, with evidence.
- `U` — where the situation is blocked or unclear.
- `L` — the simple rule or boundary that prevents damage.
- `R` — the smallest useful resolution.
- `N` — the next safe action and visible proof.

Before acting, identify the owner and what “done” looks like. Separate facts,
assumptions, and unknowns. Do not call something fixed until the person can see
or verify the result.

For a beginner, explain the capsule without jargon and offer no more than three
numbered choices.
