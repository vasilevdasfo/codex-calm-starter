# Privacy

## Safe by default

- This repository contains a blank template, not a personal profile.
- The filled `ABOUT_ME.md` stays local and is ignored by Git.
- No account connectors, MCP servers, hooks, automations, telemetry, or remote
  access are configured by this starter.
- Skills provide instructions only. They do not send or upload data by
  themselves.

## Do not store

Do not put passwords, one-time codes, private keys, payment details, identity
documents, medical records, or another person's private messages in the
workspace.

## External actions

Before sending, publishing, purchasing, connecting an account, changing access,
or deleting data, Codex must show the exact action and wait for explicit
confirmation.

## If you fork this repository

Keep `ABOUT_ME.md` ignored. Before every commit, review the changed files and
confirm that no personal notes or credentials are included.
